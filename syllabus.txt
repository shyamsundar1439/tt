## How to use this plan

- **Goal:** Strong conceptual base + complete syllabus once + start PYQs/mocks.
- **Daily time:** Aim for **6–8 hours** on weekdays, **8–10 hours** on weekends if possible. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)
- **Structure per day (example):**
  - 2–2.5 h Maths  
  - 1.5–2 h Reasoning  
  - 1.5–2 h English  
  - 1 h GK/GS (static + current)  
  - 1 h revision / PYQ / error notebook  

Adjust timings to your college schedule, but keep the **subject order and weekly targets** intact.

## Month 1 (Sep): Foundation – Arithmetic + Core Grammar + Basic Reasoning

**Focus:** Build basics, speed in calculation, and daily vocab/GA habit. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)

### Maths (Arithmetic base)
- Number system, divisibility, LCM/HCF  
- Simplification, approximation, BODMAS  
- Percentage  
- Ratio & proportion, partnership (basic)  
- Average  
- Profit & loss, discount  
- Simple interest  

**Target:** 40–50 quality questions/day from these chapters. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1011093136/SSC-CGL-4-Month-Study-Planner)

### Reasoning (High‑weight basics)
- Analogy & classification (odd one out)  
- Series (number, alphabet, mixed)  
- Coding–decoding (basic types)  
- Direction sense  
- Blood relations (basic)  

**Target:** 30–40 reasoning Q/day; maintain an error log for patterns you miss. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1011093136/SSC-CGL-4-Month-Study-Planner)

### English (Grammar + Vocab foundation)
- Parts of speech (noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb)  
- Tenses (present, past, future forms)  
- Subject–verb agreement  
- Articles, prepositions (basic rules)  
- Daily vocab: **20–25 words** (synonyms, antonyms, one‑word substitutions, idioms)  

**Target:** 1 grammar chapter/day + vocab revision every night. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1011093136/SSC-CGL-4-Month-Study-Planner)

### GK/GS (Static base + current)
- **History:** Ancient & Medieval India (key dynasties, battles, culture)  
- **Geography:** India – physical features, rivers, climate, soils  
- **Polity:** Constitution basics, Preamble, Fundamental Rights/Duties, Parliament structure  
- **Current affairs:** Last 3–4 months (national, schemes, appointments, sports, awards)  

**Target:** 30–40 min static + 20–30 min current daily; make short notes. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

## Month 2 (Oct): Complete Arithmetic + Advance Maths Start + Reasoning Core + English Core

**Focus:** Finish arithmetic, start advanced maths lightly; strengthen reasoning & English grammar. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)

### Maths
**Finish Arithmetic:**
- Compound interest  
- Time & work, pipes & cisterns  
- Time, speed & distance; trains, boats & streams  
- Mixture & alligation  
- Basic statistics: mean, median, mode; simple DI (tables, bar/line graphs)  

**Start Advanced (light):**
- Algebra: identities, linear equations, basic quadratic  
- Geometry: triangles, circles (basic theorems), lines & angles  
- Mensuration 2D (square, rectangle, triangle, circle)  

**Target:** 50–60 Q/day (mix of arithmetic + 10–15 advanced). [pw](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/ssc-cgl-2026-expected-questions-based-on-previous-years)

### Reasoning
- Syllogism (Venn diagram method)  
- Statements & conclusions / assumptions (basic)  
- Mathematical reasoning / number operations  
- Non‑verbal: mirror images, water images, embedded figures  
- Paper folding & cutting (basic)  

**Target:** 35–45 Q/day; 2–3 full reasoning sets/week. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

### English
- Error spotting (tense, agreement, preposition, conjunction, parallelism)  
- Sentence improvement & rearrangement (para jumbles basics)  
- Fill in the blanks (grammar + vocab based)  
- Cloze test (short passages)  
- Continue **20–25 vocab/day** + revision of old words  

**Target:** 1 grammar topic + 1 comprehension/cloze daily. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

### GK/GS
- **History:** Modern India (1857–1947), freedom struggle, important sessions, movements  
- **Geography:** Indian economy basics, agriculture, minerals, industries, transport  
- **Polity:** President, PM, Council of Ministers, Judiciary, Elections, Amendments (important ones)  
- **Science:** Basic physics (units, motion, force), chemistry (atoms, bonds, acids/bases), biology (cell, human systems)  
- **Current:** Ongoing 3–4 months  

**Target:** Rotate subjects daily; weekly mini‑test on static GK. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

## Month 3 (Nov): Advanced Maths + Full Reasoning Syllabus + English Advanced + GA Depth

**Focus:** Complete full syllabus once; shift more time to Tier‑2 level difficulty. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)

### Maths (Advanced heavy)
- Algebra: quadratic equations, polynomials, inequalities, graphs of linear equations  
- Geometry: triangles (similarity, congruence), circles (chords, tangents), quadrilaterals  
- Mensuration 2D & 3D: cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere, hemisphere, frustum  
- Trigonometry: ratios, identities, height & distance (basic)  
- Statistics & probability: mean, median, mode, standard deviation idea, basic probability  
- DI: tables, bar/line/pie charts, mixed DI sets  

**Target:** 60–70 Q/day, with at least 2 DI sets and 1 advanced set daily. [pw](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/ssc-cgl-2026-expected-questions-based-on-previous-years)

### Reasoning (Complete list)
- Puzzles & seating arrangement (linear, circular – basic to moderate)  
- Data sufficiency (numeric & logical)  
- Logical Venn diagrams, syllogism (advanced)  
- Statement–argument, statement–course of action (basic)  
- Non‑verbal: figural series, classification, pattern completion  
- Cube & dice (basic), calendars, clocks (selective practice)  

**Target:** 40–50 Q/day + 2 full reasoning mocks/week. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

### English
- Reading comprehension (longer passages, inference‑based)  
- Para jumbles / sentence rearrangement (moderate–hard)  
- Active–passive voice (all tenses)  
- Direct–indirect speech  
- One‑word substitution, idioms & phrases (advanced lists)  
- Spelling errors, punctuation (within error‑spotting practice)  

**Target:** 1 RC + 1 grammar/usage set daily; vocab revision every day. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

### GK/GS
- **History:** Post‑independence India (important events, PMs, wars, reforms)  
- **Geography:** World geography basics (continents, oceans, climate, important places)  
- **Polity:** Local self‑government, constitutional bodies, important committees, recent amendments  
- **Economy:** Budget terms, inflation, RBI basics, five‑year plans (overview), schemes  
- **Science:** Human body systems, diseases, nutrition, environment & ecology basics  
- **Current:** Last 6–8 months before Dec; focus on national + important international  

**Target:** Daily 45–60 min static + 30 min current; weekly GA mock. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

## Month 4 (Dec): Revision + PYQs + Full Mocks + Weak‑Area Fixing

**Focus:** Consolidate, increase speed & accuracy, simulate exam conditions. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)

### Weekly structure (Dec)
- **4 days:**  
  - 1 full Tier‑1 mock (or Tier‑2 pattern mock if you’re aiming strong for Tier‑2 too)  
  - Deep analysis (2–3 h): note every mistake, revise concept, re‑solve wrong Qs.  
- **2 days:**  
  - Subject‑wise mocks/sectional tests (Maths, Reasoning, English, GA separately).  
- **1 day:**  
  - Light study: formula sheet, vocab, GA notes, error notebook; no heavy new topics. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/978905300/SSC-CGL-4-Month-8-10H-Weekly-Timetable)

### Subject‑wise in December

**Maths:**
- Revise formulae: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, DI.  
- Solve **previous year papers (at least last 5 years)** topic‑wise first, then full papers. [pw](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/ssc-cgl-2026-expected-questions-based-on-previous-years)
- Focus on weak chapters identified from mocks (e.g., CI, geometry theorems, DI sets).

**Reasoning:**
- Daily 1 sectional test (30–45 min).  
- Revise all high‑weight topics: analogy, series, coding, syllogism, puzzles, non‑verbal.  
- Practice speed sets: 20–25 Q in 15–20 min. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

**English:**
- Daily: 1 RC + 1 grammar/usage set + vocab revision.  
- Revise rules for: error spotting, sentence improvement, voice, narration, para jumbles.  
- Solve PYQs for English (Tier‑1 & Tier‑2) to see repeated patterns. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

**GK/GS:**
- Revise your static notes: history timelines, geography facts, polity articles/bodies, science one‑liners.  
- Current affairs: last **6–9 months** before your exam; revise monthly capsules.  
- Give 2–3 GA sectional mocks/week. [adda247](https://www.adda247.com/jobs/ssc-cgl-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2026/)

## Minimal book/resource list (keep it tight)

Use **1 main + 1 practice** per subject; don’t jump between many books. [class24](https://www.class24.study/ssc-cgl/ssc-cgl-topic-wise-weightage)

- **Maths:**  
  - Concept: any one SSC‑focused Quant book (Arithmetic + Advanced)  
  - Practice: Previous year questions book (Kiran/Pinnacle/etc.) + mocks  

- **Reasoning:**  
  - One SSC reasoning book (verbal + non‑verbal)  
  - PYQ book / online question bank  

- **English:**  
  - Grammar: one standard SSC English grammar book  
  - Vocab: one “black book”‑type vocabulary/idioms/one‑word substitution  
  - RC & cloze: via mocks & PYQs  

- **GK/GS:**  
  - Static: one SSC general awareness book (Lucent‑type or equivalent)  
  - Current: monthly PDFs/capsules + a reliable news source/app  

Add **Computer Knowledge** basics in Nov–Dec (only 30–45 min/day) if you plan for Tier‑2: computer fundamentals, MS Office, internet, networking, cybersecurity basics.

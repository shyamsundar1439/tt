## 1. Scientific Audit: What Was Wrong with the Rough Routine?

The original rough routine had great intentions but contained several hidden cognitive and biological failure points that inevitably lead to burnout, procrastination, or poor retention:

| Dimension | Rough Routine | Cognitive Flaw / Biological Risk | Scientific Optimization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5:00 AM – 6:30 AM (Maths)** | 90 continuous minutes immediately after waking | **Sleep Inertia & Cold-Start Trap:** The brain takes 15–25 minutes to clear adenosine and reach high alertness. Forcing high-difficulty math at 5:00 AM causes mental drag. Continuous 90m exceeds working memory capacity. | **Warmup + Dual Sprints:** 5:15 AM formula retrieval (10m) + Sprint 1 (40m concept/problems) + 5m physical stretch + Sprint 2 (35m timed PYQs). |
| **College Gaps (9 AM – 4 PM)** | Flexible between English & History | **Decision Paralysis:** Vague "alternate between them" leads to scrolling social media because choosing what to study when tired takes too much cognitive effort. | **Micro-Sprint Decision Matrix:** 3 concrete time-based tiers (15m, 30m, 45m) with 1-click active recall prompts. |
| **4:00 PM – 6:30 PM** | Unstructured gap after college | **Energy Cliff & Procrastination Zone:** Returning from a 7-hour college schedule leaves the prefrontal cortex depleted. If unstructured, low-value scrolling fills the void. | **Decompression & Priming Buffer:** 4:00–5:30 PM travel, nourishment, physical walk/rest; 5:30–6:00 PM casual review/tea. Clean 6:30 PM start. |
| **6:30 PM – 7:30 PM (Reasoning)** | 60m Reasoning | Good placement, but needs tight feedback loops. | **High-Dopamine Speed Blocks:** 35–40 questions in timed sets + immediate error log for missed patterns. |
| **7:30 PM – 9:00 PM (History)** | 90m History after Reasoning | **Cognitive Asymmetry & Passive Reading:** Doing 90m of rote History in the evening after already doing History during college gaps neglects General Science, Polity, Geography, and Current Affairs! Furthermore, working memory is exhausted by 8:00 PM. | **Interleaved GA Rotation & English Cloze:** 7:45–8:15 PM GK/GS rotation (Polity, History, Science as per syllabus) + 8:15–8:40 PM English application (Cloze/Error rules). |
| **8:45 PM – 9:00 PM** | No designated wind-down | Brain stays stimulated; leads to delayed sleep onset and ruined 5:00 AM wakeups. | **Low-Cognitive Active Recall & 60-Sec Check-out:** Review today's mistake notebook + log wins + prime tomorrow. |

## 2. The Optimized Master Schedule

### 🌅 Block 1: Morning Prime & Deep Quant Focus (5:15 AM – 6:45 AM)
*Target Mental State: Peak Alertness & Analytical Processing*
* **5:15 AM – 5:25 AM (10 mins) — Neuro-Priming:** Hydration, light stretching, and rapid retrieval of arithmetic formulas or tables/squares.
* **5:25 AM – 6:05 AM (40 mins) — Focus Sprint 1:** Core conceptual study & 20 guided problem variations (Percentage / Ratio / Number system).
* **6:05 AM – 6:10 AM (5 mins) — Strategic Oxygen Break:** Stand up, drink water, no screens.
* **6:10 AM – 6:45 AM (35 mins) — Focus Sprint 2:** 20 timed PYQs under exam constraints + note any mistakes in the error notebook.
* **Reward:** +60 XP points.

---

### 🎓 Block 2: College Micro-Sprints (Between 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM)
*Target Mental State: Low Friction, Bite-Sized Retrieval*
Never carry heavy textbooks to college. Use the dashboard on mobile:
* **Option A: 10–15 Min Window (The "Between Classes" Sprint)**
  * *English:* 20 high-frequency vocab words (Synonyms/Antonyms/One-word substitutions) using the built-in flashcard deck.
  * *History/GK:* Rapid recall of dynasty capitals, Buddhist councils, or constitutional articles.
* **Option B: 20–30 Min Window (The "Free Period" Sprint)**
  * *English:* Solve 1 Cloze Test passage (10 Qs) + check 5 tricky grammar rules.
  * *History/GK:* Review Modern India timeline (INC sessions 1885–1947) or static physical geography maps.
* **Option C: 30–60 Min Window (The "Library / Cancelled Class" Sprint)**
  * Deep comprehension passage or 30 Lucent static GK questions.
* **Reward:** +25 to +45 XP per micro-sprint.

---

### ☕ Transition & Decompression (4:00 PM – 6:15 PM)
* **4:00 PM – 5:15 PM:** Commute home, shower, high-protein snack, complete cognitive rest (zero guilt).
* **5:15 PM – 6:00 PM:** Light walk, social connection, or casual personal time.
* **6:00 PM – 6:15 PM:** Setup study desk, water bottle ready, eliminate phone distractions.

---

### 🧩 Block 3: Evening High-Engagement Logic (6:30 PM – 7:30 PM)
*Target Mental State: Active Problem Solving with Instant Feedback*
* Why Reasoning works best here: Solving puzzles, series, and coding-decoding provides immediate dopamine hits that cut through post-college fatigue.
* **Format:** 35–40 questions from high-weight topics (Analogy, Coding-decoding, Syllogism, Direction sense).
* Maintain an **Error Log** for every missed pattern (e.g., reverse alphabet shifts or alternate prime series).
* **Reward:** +50 XP points.

---

### 🧘 Evening Transition (7:30 PM – 7:45 PM)
* 15-minute break. Tea, light movement, transition from analytical to verbal/memory modes.

---

### 📚 Block 4: Interleaved GK & English Application (7:45 PM – 8:45 PM)
*Target Mental State: Associative Memory & Verbal Retention*
* **7:45 PM – 8:15 PM (30 mins) — GK/GS Rotation:**
  * *Mon/Wed:* History (Ancient & Medieval in Sep, Modern in Oct, Post-Independence in Nov).
  * *Tue/Thu:* Polity (Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Articles).
  * *Fri:* Geography & Science basics.
  * *Sat:* Current Affairs monthly capsule.
* **8:15 PM – 8:45 PM (30 mins) — English Practice:**
  * Error-spotting drill (15 Qs) or 1 Reading Comprehension passage.
* **Reward:** +45 XP points.

---

### 🌙 Block 5: Wind-Down & Error Retrieval (8:45 PM – 9:00 PM)
*Target Mental State: Consolidation & Psychological Closure*
* **8:45 PM – 8:55 PM (10 mins):** Review today's **Mistake Notebook** (active recall of formulas/words missed earlier today).
* **8:55 PM – 9:00 PM (5 mins):** Launch the **60-Second Night Review** on the dashboard.
  * Check off completed tasks.
  * Missed something? Reschedule it to tomorrow with 1-click (Zero guilt!).
  * Note tomorrow's #1 priority.
* **Reward:** +30 XP bonus & streak extension.
* **Sleep by 10:15–10:30 PM** to ensure a full 7 hours of restorative sleep before the 5:15 AM session.

## 3. The 4-Month Progression Map (Aligned with `syllabus.txt`)

* **Month 1 (September): Foundation & Calculation Speed**
  * *Maths:* Number system, BODMAS, Percentage, Ratio, Average, P&L, SI. (40–50 Qs/day).
  * *Reasoning:* Analogy, Series, Coding-decoding, Direction, Blood relations (30–40 Qs/day).
  * *English:* Core grammar rules (Parts of speech, Tenses, Agreement) + 25 Vocab/day.
  * *GK/GS:* Ancient & Medieval India, Physical Geography, Polity Basics, Current Affairs.
* **Month 2 (October): Complete Arithmetic + Advance Maths Kickoff**
  * *Maths:* CI, Time & Work, TSD, Mixture + Start Algebra & Geometry (50–60 Qs/day).
  * *Reasoning:* Syllogisms (Venn diagrams), Non-verbal, Statement & conclusions.
  * *English:* Error spotting, Cloze tests, Sentence improvement.
  * *GK/GS:* Modern India (1857–1947), Indian Economy, Executive/Judiciary, Science basics.
* **Month 3 (November): Advanced Heavy + Tier-2 Difficulty**
  * *Maths:* Quadratic equations, Geometry similarity/circles, Mensuration 3D, Trigonometry, DI.
  * *Reasoning:* Seating arrangement, Puzzles, Data sufficiency.
  * *English:* Long Reading Comprehension, Voice & Narration, Advanced Idioms.
  * *GK/GS:* Post-independence, World Geography, Economics, Diseases & Ecology.
* **Month 4 (December): Full Mocks + Speed + Weak Area Remediation**
  * *4 Days/Week:* Full Tier-1/Tier-2 Mocks + 2.5h Deep Error Analysis.
  * *2 Days/Week:* Sectional tests (Maths & Reasoning speed drills: 25 Qs in 18 mins).
  * *1 Day/Week:* Formula sheet, Vocab flashcard marathons, Zero new heavy topics.

3. **Sound & Confetti Dopamine Triggers:**
   * Built-in Web Audio chime + particle confetti on task completion to deliver an immediate neurological sense of accomplishment.

## 5. How to Launch and Use the Interactive Dashboard

### Launching the Production Server:
1. Start the backend companion server:
   ```bash
   cd /home/shyam/Projects
   python3 server.py
   ```
   * Desktop access: `http://localhost:8000`

2. **For Mobile Phone Usage (Full PWA & Notifications Support):**
   Modern mobile browsers (Chrome / Safari) require a Secure Context (HTTPS) to allow Service Workers and Mobile Web Notifications over Wi-Fi. Run with `--ssl`:
   ```bash
   cd /home/shyam/Projects
   python3 server.py --ssl
   ```
   * Open the displayed LAN URL on your phone (e.g. `https://192.168.1.184:8000`).
   * Tap **"Add to Home screen"** in your mobile browser to install it as an app.
   * Tap **"Enable Mobile Notifications"** in the app.
   * Tap **"Test Buzz & Sound"** to verify that notifications, vibrations, and audio chimes work on your phone!

3. **Daily Routine Workflow:**
   * **Morning (5:15 AM):** Receive morning Quant reminder. Tap **"Check-in"** to log energy and sleep.
   * **During College (9:00 AM – 4:00 PM):** Open **College Free-Period Recommendation**, choose 15m/30m/45m, and start a bite-sized active recall sprint.
   * **Evening (6:30 PM & 7:45 PM):** High-engagement Reasoning block and GK/English drill. Focus timer buzzer alerts you when done while keeping your phone screen awake.
   * **Night (8:45 PM):** Click **"Review"** to complete the 60-Second Wind-Down and rollover any missed tasks with zero guilt.
